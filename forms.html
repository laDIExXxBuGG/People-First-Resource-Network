Prepare before filling out a form

  • ☐ Confirm that the form comes from the official agency, court, or organization.
  • ☐ Check the form's version, date, and instructions.
  • ☐ Write down the deadline and required submission method.
  • ☐ Gather the documents specifically requested by the instructions.
  • ☐ Identify questions you do not understand before submitting.
  • ☐ Keep a copy of the completed form and supporting documents.
  • ☐ Save proof of submission, such as a receipt, confirmation number, or tracking record.
  • ☐ If you do not know an answer, check the instructions or ask the receiving organization rather than guessing.

When paperwork becomes confusing

I do not understand a question

Break the question into smaller parts. Look for definitions in the official instructions and ask the organization to clarify anything that remains unclear.

I am missing a document

Ask whether an alternative document is accepted, how to request a replacement, and whether you can submit available documents while obtaining the missing item.

I cannot complete it online

Check whether a paper form, accessible format, alternate submission method, or free assistance is available.

My deadline is approaching

Review the official deadline instructions promptly. Ask the receiving organization about submission rules, extensions, accommodations, or other available options. Keep a record.

Your paperwork preparation worksheet

Use these prompts in your own notes before contacting an agency or requesting help.

  1. What am I applying for or responding to?
  2. Who issued the form, and where must it go?
  3. What is the deadline?
  4. Which documents do I already have?
  5. What information or documents are missing?
  6. What question must be answered before I submit it?
  7. How will I keep proof of submission?

Keep this worksheet somewhere private. Do not enter sensitive personal information into public website fields or posts.

Find another way

If the first route does not work, that does not mean you are out of options.

  • Ask the organization for plain-language instructions.
  • Ask whether free application assistance is available.
  • Ask if an alternative document or submission method is accepted.
  • For legal filings, contact legal aid or the relevant court's self-help service.
  • Keep a record of deadlines, messages, and instructions you receive.
  • If you are denied, ask for the written reason and instructions for any review or appeal, including its deadline.
