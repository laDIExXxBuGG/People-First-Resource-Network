Preparing for a Court Matter

  1. Identify the court, jurisdiction, case type, and case number.
  2. Read the most recent court order, notice, or filing carefully.
  3. Write down all deadlines and hearing dates.
  4. Organize relevant documents and create a factual timeline.
  5. Find the official court rules and forms for your jurisdiction.
  6. Contact legal aid or a qualified lawyer when possible, especially if a deadline or legal right is at risk.
  7. Keep copies of everything filed and proof of submission or service.

What if you cannot afford a lawyer?

Ask legal aid organizations about eligibility, advice clinics, self-help centers, limited-scope representation, and referrals. Availability varies by location and case type.

If one service cannot help, ask whether it can refer you to another organization or explain what alternatives are available.

Important legal information

This website provides general educational information and resource navigation, not individualized legal advice or attorney representation. Laws, forms, deadlines, filing requirements, and court procedures vary by jurisdiction and can change.

Always verify current requirements with the official court or a qualified legal professional. Do not ignore a court notice or deadline while searching for help.
