Preparing to Apply

Requirements vary by program. Before applying, consider preparing:

  • Identification and contact information.
  • Household details, if the program requests them.
  • Income, employment, or benefit information, if required.
  • Housing, utility, or medical expense documents, if applicable.
  • Any program-specific forms and supporting evidence.

Do not send sensitive documents to this website. Submit them only through the responsible agency's verified process.

What if the first option doesn't work?

You may still have other routes to explore. Ask the agency about alternative programs, missing documents, reconsideration or appeal rights, and deadlines. Eligibility and appeal procedures vary.

If an application is denied, read the notice carefully and check the deadline before deciding what to do next.
