About the Project

People First Resource Network is a free, public resource and guidance platform designed to help individuals and families navigate complicated systems, find assistance, understand their options, and take practical next steps.

Whether someone is facing financial hardship, disability-related barriers, housing instability, legal paperwork, educational challenges, mental health concerns, or everyday needs, finding help shouldn't require knowing exactly where to look or how complicated systems work.

Our mission is to make reliable information easier to understand, resources easier to find, and alternative paths easier to discover when the first option doesn't work.

Our Guiding Principle

There Is Always Another Step.

When a program is unavailable, an application is denied, a waiting list is too long, transportation is a barrier, or a resource is unaffordable, people deserve to understand their alternatives rather than reach a dead end.

We cannot guarantee that every problem will have an immediate solution, but we aim to help people identify realistic options and understand what they can try next.

Our 13 Divisions

1. Benefits & Basic Needs — Food, healthcare, benefits, housing assistance, and financial support.

2. Pro Se Legal Support — Court procedures, self-representation information, legal aid, and civil court resources.

3. Forms & Paperwork Help — Plain-language form guidance, checklists, templates, and application preparation.

4. Education & Homeschooling — Homeschool requirements, learning materials, educational options, and family learning support.

5. Disability & Accessibility Center — Disability benefits, accommodations, accessibility, advocacy, and support.

6. Further Education, Skills & Career Pathways — Training, scholarships, vocational programs, certifications, and career planning.

7. Small Business & Independent Workers — Business resources, registration guidance, training, funding opportunities, and self-employment support.

8. Pet Care & Animal Wellbeing — Affordable veterinary care, pet food assistance, preventive care, and animal welfare resources.

9. Everyday Essentials, Connectivity & Transportation — Internet, phones, clothing, transportation, vehicle ownership, and consumer guidance.

10. Homelessness, Housing Crisis & Survival Support — Emergency assistance, housing resources, essential services, and safer alternatives.

11. Pregnancy, Parenthood & Family Planning — Reproductive health information, pregnancy support, parenting resources, and family assistance.

12. Mental Health & Emotional Wellbeing — Mental health education, affordable care options, support services, and practical coping tools.

13. Local & Community Resource Finder — Community organizations, local services, nonprofit programs, and regional resources.

What We Aim to Provide

- Free-first and affordable resource discovery.
- Clear eligibility requirements, costs, deadlines, and application instructions.
- Official links and reliable sources whenever possible.
- Plain-language explanations and practical paperwork tools.
- Local, statewide, national, and online options.
- Accessible, mobile-friendly information.
- Alternatives when a resource is unavailable, inaccessible, or unsuitable.
- Transparent verification dates and known limitations.

Our Commitment

People first. Privacy matters. Access should not depend on income.

The project aims to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information, make browsing available without requiring an account, and present information in a respectful, accessible, and understandable way.

Resources will be identified as carefully as possible, with official sources prioritized. Availability, eligibility, costs, and rules may change, so users should verify important details with the responsible organization.

Legal and medical information is intended for education and resource navigation. It does not replace individualized advice from a qualified professional.

Project Status

In development.

The initial goal is to establish the website structure, organize the 13 divisions, and gradually build a useful directory of verified resources and practical guides.

The project will be developed in stages, with accessibility, privacy, reliability, and free public access as guiding priorities.

Technology

The initial website is intended to use accessible, mobile-friendly web technologies and free hosting where available.
